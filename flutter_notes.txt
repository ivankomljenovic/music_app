Basic Widgets to Know:

1. Container
-has properties like 
    color: change child's background colour
    padding: make the background larger
    margin: add empty space around the background
    decoration: change the background shape (fitting to narowest parameter)
    alignment: sets alignment of child within the container
    width, height: sets dimensions of the container
    transform: rotate or perform a transform on the container
